Publish student and family names, photos, and stories only when consent has been confirmed and an administrator has approved the entry.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected consent plus admin approval as the rule for public student and family pages.

**How to apply:** Keep unapproved records out of public lists and video feeds, and do not expose identifying fields until both conditions are satisfied.
